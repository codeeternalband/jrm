Juho Ranta-Maunus (born June 29, 2006) is a -year-old guitarist from Finland.

Juho has studied guitar and music theory for more than a decade, alongside piano, ensemble work and performance studies. He has also taken part in summer programs at Berklee College of Music and Musicians Institute. He holds artist endorsements with Ibanez, Neural DSP and Levytukku. Among his most notable appearances are performances as a special guest with Finland's largest live production, Raskasta Joulua, and on the Elämäni Biisi (Song Of My Life) TV show in 2026.

He is the founder of the Finnish progressive metal band Code Eternal, where he works as lead guitarist, composer and arranger. He has also worked with melodic metalcore band Averse.

His playing was recognized internationally after winning Guitar World magazine’s Young Guitarist of the Year in 2020, and he has continued to build his path through solo releases, band work and live performances.

He will begin his studies in the performance program at Metropolia University of Applied Sciences in the fall of 2026. He is currently working on Code Eternal's debut album, and new solo material will also be released during 2026.
