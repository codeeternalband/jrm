Bio

Juho Ranta-Maunus (born June 29, 2006) is a guitarist from Finland. His playing was recognized internationally after winning Guitar World magazine’s Young Guitarist of the Year in 2020, and he has continued to build his path through solo releases, band work, live performances and music production.

He is the founder of the Finnish progressive metal band Code Eternal, where he works as lead guitarist, composer and arranger, and he has also worked with the band Averse. Alongside his own solo material, his work has included original releases, performances in Finland and abroad, and a growing presence on YouTube and social media.

Juho has studied guitar and music theory for more than a decade, alongside piano, band playing and performance studies. His development has also included programs at Berklee College of Music and Musicians Institute. He has artist endorsements with Ibanez, Neural DSP and Levytukku, and has received several scholarships and recognitions along the way.
