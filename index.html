Biography

Juho "JRM" Ranta-Maunus is a Finnish guitarist known for precision playing, progressive phrasing and a strong modern metal sound.

At just 14 years old, Juho won Guitar World Magazine’s “Young Guitarist of the Year” in 2020. Since then, he has continued building his identity as a player, writer and creative force with a style that combines technical control, melody and intensity.

This site highlights Juho’s work, music and current releases.
