Juho Ranta-Maunus (born June 29, 2006) is a -year-old guitarist from Finland.

Juho has studied guitar and music theory for more than a decade. He holds artist endorsements with Ibanez, Neural DSP and Levytukku. Notable appearances include Raskasta Joulua and the Elämäni Biisi TV show in 2026.

He is the founder of Code Eternal and has worked with Averse. He was named Guitar World’s Young Guitarist of the Year in 2020.
